How the Assessment Works

From sign-up to a doctor-reviewed result, in three steps.

1

Create Your Account

Register yourself and choose your doctor — or your care team can add you directly — so your history stays connected to them from day one.

2

Complete Your Questionnaire

Answer an adaptive cognitive questionnaire that adjusts each question to what you just said. If an MRI is needed, your doctor handles the scan and its analysis directly.

3

Review by Your Doctor

Your doctor reviews the AI-drafted findings — MRI classification and cognitive summary alike — before anything reaches you. A screening aid, never a standalone diagnosis.

Built for Patients and Care Teams

One platform, two perspectives.

For Patients

  • An adaptive questionnaire that follows up on your own answers — no fixed script, just what's relevant to you
  • Clear, non-alarming results with a plain-language summary
  • A private space to revisit past results over time
  • Confidence that your doctor reviews every result before you see it

For Healthcare Professionals

  • A centralized dashboard of registered patients and their AI-assisted results
  • Explainable MRI analysis — heatmaps highlighting exactly which regions drove each prediction
  • An AI-drafted clinical report, grounded in retrieved medical guidance, that you can edit before releasing
  • A faster way to flag patients who may need further evaluation
